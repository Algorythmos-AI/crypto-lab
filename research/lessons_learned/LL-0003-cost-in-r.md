# LL-0003: Cost is measured in R, and it has to be measured

Two decision records said a crypto trade started "half an R down" because stops were "1% to 2%". That was the
smallest stop allowed, not the stop used: measured, the median stop was 3.9% and the cost 0.25R (DEC-0022).
The wrong figure shaped two experiments. Rule: every backtest report states the mean cost in R and the mean R
before costs, computed from the trades, beside the result.
