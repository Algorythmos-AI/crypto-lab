# LL-0006: Describe the test window from its prices, not from memory

An audit of the crypto desk called its two-year window "one bull period with no bear market". The window's
own prices show every coin rising 90% to 575% and then falling 54% to 89%. The claim was written before the
buy-and-hold line existed. Rule: a report carries the return and drawdown of simply holding the instruments
over the same span, and any sentence about the regime is checked against it.
