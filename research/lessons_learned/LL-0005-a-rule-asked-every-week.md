# LL-0005: A selection rule that is asked every week will say yes by chance

"Choose a model if it beats the baseline" was run on data 28 hours apart and gave no candidate, then a
candidate whose margin was a fifth of its standard error (DEC-0026). A calibrated model with no information is
the baseline plus noise, so it lands on either side about half the time. Rule: a recurring selection needs a
margin, and the margin is stated in the same record as the schedule.
